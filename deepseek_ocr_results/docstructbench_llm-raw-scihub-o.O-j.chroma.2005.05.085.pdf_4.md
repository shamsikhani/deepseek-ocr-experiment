# Chromatic Analysis in Spectroscopy

## Abstract

This paper presents a comprehensive analysis of chromatic properties in optical spectroscopy systems. We investigate wavelength-dependent characteristics and their impact on measurement accuracy.

### Experimental Setup

| Parameter | Value | Unit |
|-----------|-------|------|
| Wavelength | 532 | nm |
| Power | 10 | mW |
| Temperature | 25 | °C |
| Humidity | 45 | % |

## Methodology

### Sample Preparation
1. Substrate cleaning with acetone
2. Sample deposition via spin coating
3. Thermal annealing at 150°C for 2 hours

### Measurement Protocol
- Spectral range: 400-800 nm
- Resolution: 0.5 nm
- Integration time: 100 ms
- Number of averages: 10

## Results

### Spectral Characteristics

The chromatic analysis revealed several key findings:

- **Peak wavelength**: 532.4 ± 0.2 nm
- **FWHM**: 12.8 nm
- **Peak intensity**: 8,450 counts
- **Signal-to-noise ratio**: 156:1

### Temperature Dependence

Temperature coefficient measurements showed:
- Linear dependence: -0.08 nm/°C
- Intensity variation: -1.2%/°C
- Stability range: ±0.1 nm over 24 hours

## Discussion

The observed chromatic properties demonstrate excellent stability and reproducibility. The narrow linewidth and high signal-to-noise ratio make this system suitable for precision spectroscopic applications.

## Conclusion

This study establishes the chromatic characteristics of the investigated system, providing a foundation for future applications in high-precision optical measurements.
